Clothoid oval — two carriers at constant 115 mm pitch on the orange path

The blue line is the closed track centreline: two 250 mm straights joined by two 500 mm clothoid U-turns, 1500 mm per lap. The orange line is its true parallel offset, 99 mm to the outside. Carriers A and B start on the bottom straight at 65 mm and 180 mm along the orange path and hold that 115 mm spacing right around the loop. Master zero is marked 0 in the drawing, where the left curve meets the bottom straight; the path is measured from there, rightwards along the bottom first.

Cam table

Master is position along the orange path, slave is position along the blue path. The lap is one straight-plus-curve unit repeated twice, so the table is periodic: it covers and TwinCAT repeats it. A and B ride that same table, offset by 115 mm of master, so one table drives both. A enters at master 65 mm, B at 180 mm, both measured from the zero mark in the drawing. The straight is Polynomial1; the curve is Polynomial5_MM, which carries a slave velocity and acceleration at each point.
